Plant APS Design工厂 APS 设计

A finite-capacity scheduler for every line in the plant that puts on-time delivery first. SAP S/4HANA stays the system of record for orders, the custom MES executes the schedule, and the smart-factory platform feeds live machine, quality and maintenance status. A constraint solver builds the schedule; Laya turns written disruptions into typed events; the planner approves what is published.

面向全厂所有产线的有限产能排程系统，以准时交付为第一目标。SAP S/4HANA 仍是订单的主数据系统，定制 MES 负责执行排程，智能工厂平台实时提供设备、质量和维护状态。排程由约束求解器生成；Laya 把文字形式的异常转成结构化事件；发布前由计划员审批。

1Goals and scope目标与范围

The APS schedules every order through every line of Plant 1 against real capacity, and keeps that schedule right when machines stop, material is late or quality holds a lot.

APS 按真实产能为一号工厂所有产线的全部订单排程，并在设备停机、物料延迟或质量冻结时及时修正排程。

Goal目标Proposed target建议指标
On-time delivery (OTD)准时交付率 (OTD)Maximise; baseline to be measured against today's manual plan尽可能提高；以当前人工计划为基线实测
Reschedule after a disruption异常后重排Proposal ready in under 30 s; published after planner approval30 秒内给出方案；计划员审批后发布
Schedule stability排程稳定性Nothing inside the frozen window moves; outside it, as few operations as possible冻结窗口内不变动；窗口外尽量少调整
Traceability可追溯Every published schedule keeps its inputs, events, objective values and approver每次发布的排程都保留输入、事件、目标值和审批人

In scope范围内

Detailed scheduling of production orders across all lines; event-driven rescheduling; what-if scenarios; dispatch lists to the MES; scheduled dates back to SAP.

全产线生产订单的详细排程；事件驱动重排；假设场景分析；向 MES 下发派工单；排程日期回写 SAP。

Not in scope范围外

MRP and purchasing stay in SAP. The APS never releases or changes orders in SAP on its own, and never controls a machine.

MRP 与采购仍在 SAP 中完成。APS 不会自行下达或修改 SAP 订单，也不直接控制设备。

5Scheduling model排程模型

A flexible job shop solved with Google OR-Tools CP-SAT. On-time delivery is optimised first and locked in before anything else is considered.

采用柔性作业车间模型，由 Google OR-Tools CP-SAT 求解。先优化准时交付并锁定结果，再考虑其他目标。

2. minimise  Σ setup minutes   s.t. level 1 ≤ best found

Co is the order's completion time, do its due date and wo its priority weight from SAP. Each level is solved and its value locked before the next, so a setup saving can never cost a late order.

Co 为订单完工时间，do 为交期，wo 为来自 SAP 的优先级权重。逐级求解并锁定上一级结果，因此节省换型永远不会以订单延误为代价。

Changed while building the demo实现过程中的调整

Draft 0.1 re-optimised the whole plan on every event within 15 s. Measured, that was too slow: the setup sequencing makes a 150-operation plan hard, and repairs often stopped at poor solutions. Restricting a repair to its neighbourhood, and pruning sequence choices the neighbourhood already fixes, made repairs reach a proven optimum for on-time delivery in 1–30 s. Repairs therefore run for up to 30 s, and setups are optimised only by a full plan.

草案 0.1 在每个事件后于 15 秒内重新优化全部排程。实测过慢：换型顺序使 150 道工序的问题很难求解，修复常停在较差的解。将修复限定在邻域内，并剪除邻域已确定的顺序选择后，修复可在 1–30 秒内证明准时交付级的最优解。因此修复时限改为 30 秒，换型只在全量排程中优化。

7Laya and LLM rolesLaya 与大模型分工

The solver decides the schedule. Laya reads what people write: disruptions, commands, rejection comments and shift notes. Rules find the facts and the cues Laya cannot read. The schedule explains itself: delay causes are computed, not generated. An LLM is kept for free-form requests the rules cannot parse. The same rule as the quality platform: models read, rules and solvers decide, people approve.

排程由求解器决定。Laya 理解人写的文字：异常、命令、驳回意见和交接班记录。规则提取事实以及 Laya 无法识别的信号。排程自己给出解释：延误原因由计算得出，而非生成。大模型留给规则无法解析的自由请求。与质量平台同一原则：模型负责理解，规则与求解器负责决策，人负责审批。

Laya questions for the disruption inbox异常收件箱的 Laya 问题

Choice questions only, as measured on the quality platform, all answered in one forward pass: the event kind read twice with different wording (one flat list of ten, and area first, then the kind within the area), and for the planner's command bar the intent (what-if, report, why). guard_questions() screen the text for prompt injection first. An event applies itself only when both readings agree, both clear 50%, the guard is quiet, the rules found every fact and the sender is sure; otherwise the planner confirms Laya's prefilled reading. Measured: either reading alone made 2–3 confident errors on 18 messages, on different messages; requiring agreement left none. Laya never produces the numbers: rules extract machines, orders, hours, start times, date shifts and quantities, and check them against master data.

沿用质量平台的实测结论，只使用选择题，一次前向计算全部回答：以两种不同措辞识别事件类型（一组十个选项的平铺列表，以及先判断领域、再判断领域内类型），以及计划助手命令的意图（假设分析、上报、原因）。先用 guard_questions() 检查提示注入。只有两次识别一致、均高于 50%、防护无告警、规则提取到全部信息且发件人确定时，事件才会自动应用；否则由计划员确认 Laya 预填的结果。实测：在 18 条消息上，任一识别单独使用会产生 2–3 条高置信度错误，且出错的消息不同；要求一致后为零。数字从不由 Laya 给出：设备、订单、小时数、开始时间、交期调整和数量由规则提取并与主数据核对。

Two more jobs for Laya, and three cues it does not readLaya 的另两项任务，以及它不负责的三类信号

Rejected proposals: Laya reads the planner's comment (too many changes, a customer promise, people or tools missing, the event was wrong) and the workbench offers the matching next step: re-run with only the disrupted machine and order free, re-run protecting orders, block a machine, or correct the event. All four stay on screen, because two of eight confident readings on held-out comments were wrong. Shift notes: Laya reads the machine's condition; a likely breakdown marks the machine on the Gantt. Rules instead of Laya read whether the sender is unsure, what a delay costs the customer, and urgency: measured on 32 new messages, Laya could not separate these, while a short word list in three languages could.

被驳回的方案：Laya 识别计划员的驳回意见（改动太多、违背客户承诺、人员或工装不足、事件有误），工作台给出对应的下一步：只放开受影响设备和订单重新求解、保护指定订单重新求解、封锁设备或修正事件。四个选项始终显示，因为在保留测试集上 8 个高置信度识别中有 2 个错误。交接班记录：Laya 识别设备状态；可能故障的设备会在甘特图上标出。由规则而非 Laya 判断发件人是否确定、延误对客户的影响以及紧急度：在 32 条新消息上实测，Laya 无法区分这些信号，而三种语言的简短词表可以。

9Planner workbench计划员工作台

One screen: a Gantt chart per machine, orders coloured by delivery risk, the disruption inbox, and a before-and-after comparison for every proposal. English and Chinese, switchable per user.

一个页面包含：按设备的甘特图、按交付风险着色的订单、异常收件箱，以及每个方案的前后对比。支持中英文，按用户切换。

  • Orders list: due date, planned finish, slack, status pill (on time, at risk, late), SAP order number.
  • Disruption inbox: incoming messages with Laya's two readings, urgency (from rules), guard result and the reason a message needs the planner; one click triages the whole inbox by urgency.
  • Command bar: “What if LKT-04 goes down for 8 hours?” runs a scenario; “Why is 10004403 late?” shows the ranked causes.
  • What-if scenarios: up to six side by side with KPI deltas; view one on the Gantt, promote it to the proposal, or discard it.
  • Why late and load: the main cause of every late or at-risk order, and a heatmap of load by work centre and day.
  • Proposal panel: OTD, weighted tardiness, late orders and setup hours before and after; operations moved; approve or reject.
  • Publish: shows the MES dispatch lists and SAP date updates that will be sent.
  • 订单列表：交期、计划完工、余量、状态（准时、有风险、延误）、SAP 订单号。
  • 异常收件箱：显示 Laya 的两次识别、紧急度（由规则判断）、防护结果以及需计划员确认的原因；一键按紧急度分拣全部消息。
  • 计划助手：“如果 CNC-02 停机 4 小时会怎样？”运行假设场景；“订单 10004407 为什么有延误风险？”显示排序后的原因。
  • 假设场景：最多六个并列，显示指标变化；可在甘特图中查看、转为方案或删除。
  • 延误原因与负荷：每个延误或有风险订单的主要原因，以及按工作中心和日期的负荷热力图。
  • 方案面板：变更前后的 OTD、加权延误、延误订单数、换型工时；被调整的工序；批准或驳回。
  • 发布：预览将发送的 MES 派工单与 SAP 日期更新。

11Deployment and security部署与安全

The APS is one more service on the plant hub, behind the same zones, sign-in and audit log as the rest of the platform.

APS 作为工厂中心上的一个服务，沿用平台的网络分区、统一登录与审计日志。

  • Runs on: the plant hub; the solver needs CPU cores, not a GPU. Laya reuses the edge or hub model service.
  • SAP access: a technical user with read rights on orders and masters, and write rights limited to scheduled dates on production orders.
  • Roles: planner (propose, approve, publish), supervisor (view, comment), administrator. Every publish records who approved it.
  • If the APS is down: the MES keeps the last published dispatch lists; the floor continues on them.
  • 运行位置：工厂中心；求解器需要 CPU 核心，不需要 GPU。Laya 复用边缘或中心的模型服务。
  • SAP 访问：技术用户，对订单与主数据只读；写权限仅限生产订单的排程日期。
  • 角色：计划员（生成方案、审批、发布）、主管（查看、评论）、管理员。每次发布都记录审批人。
  • APS 故障时：MES 保留最近一次发布的派工单，车间按其继续生产。
