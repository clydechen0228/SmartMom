How it works

1 · COLLECTSensors & PLCsBore gauge, CMM, nutrunner, leak tester, camera. Operators type notes at the station HMI.
2 · FORWARDIoT edge gatewayBatches readings with a per-device sequence number; store-and-forward with backoff when the link drops.
3 · INGESTTelemetry APIBearer auth per gateway, schema validation, duplicate drop on (device, seq), 429 backpressure. Raw payload stored first.
4 · RULESLimits & SPCTolerances, reaction plans, Western Electric rules, alarm-code table. Deterministic; the final word on numbers.
5 · LAYAReads the operator noteOne forward pass, typed questions, any language. Routed to the English or multilingual checkpoint. Records without a note skip it.
6 · DECIDEPolicy gateAuto-release only when rules pass and Laya is ≥ 50% sure the note is harmless. Everything else goes to QA, with the reasons.

Rules decide

Whether a measurement is in tolerance · the reaction plan (rework / scrap) · SPC drift · what an alarm code means. A note can never overrule a failed measurement.

Laya decides

What a person on the line wrote, in their language: a defect the sensors missed, a suspect gauge (hold instead of scrap), a bad material lot, a machine problem. Combined with SPC drift, a machine problem stops the line.
